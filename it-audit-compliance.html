Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rennie IT provide HIPAA Security Risk Assessments?

Yes. Rennie IT offers HIPAA Security Risk Assessments (SRAs) to help organizations identify risks to electronic protected health information and prioritize security improvements.

What does a HIPAA Security Risk Assessment include?

An assessment reviews relevant administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, identifies potential security gaps, and documents risks and recommended remediation actions. The exact scope is agreed upon before work begins.

Can Rennie IT help after an IT audit identifies risks?

Yes. Rennie IT can help build a risk register, prioritize findings, track remediation, and prepare supporting evidence for follow-up reviews.

Which areas does Rennie IT serve?

Rennie IT is located in Boaz, Alabama, and serves businesses in Albertville, Guntersville, Arab, Gadsden, Rainbow City, and surrounding Northeast Alabama communities.
